# SOP 05: Weekly optimisation routine

**Time:** 30 to 60 minutes per account. **Rule:** one decision per issue, logged in the change log with a reason and a date to re-check.

## Google Ads

1. **Health check (5 min)**
   - [ ] Spend pacing vs. monthly budget.
   - [ ] Any disapproved ads, limited assets, or policy notices.
   - [ ] Conversion tracking status: no actions showing *No recent conversions* unexpectedly.
2. **Search terms (15 min)**
   - [ ] Review the last 7 days' search terms by cost.
   - [ ] Add irrelevant terms as negatives at the right level.
   - [ ] Add consistently converting terms as phrase or exact keywords in the right ad group.
3. **Performance (10 min)**
   - [ ] CPA / ROAS by campaign vs. target, over a window long enough to cover conversion lag.
   - [ ] Campaigns limited by budget that are on target: candidates for more budget.
   - [ ] Campaigns over target CPA for multiple weeks: check search terms, landing page and bids before cutting.
4. **Ads (5 min)**
   - [ ] Asset performance labels on RSAs; replace the weakest headline when there's enough data.

## Meta Ads

1. [ ] Cost per result by ad set and ad vs. target.
2. [ ] Frequency and CTR trend per ad (fatigue check).
3. [ ] Learning status: anything stuck in *Learning limited* needs consolidation or more budget.
4. [ ] Launch at least one new creative concept every one to two weeks for accounts with steady spend.

## Both

- [ ] Lead quality check with the client: a sample of leads or the CRM's qualified-lead count.
- [ ] Update the change log.

## Things not to do weekly

- Don't change Smart Bidding targets every week; give each change time to settle.
- Don't judge last few days' CPA without accounting for conversion lag.
- Don't pause a campaign because of one bad week without checking tracking first.
