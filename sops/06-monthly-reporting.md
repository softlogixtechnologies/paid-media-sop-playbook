# SOP 06: Monthly client reporting

**Goal:** a report the client reads in five minutes and acts on. **Template:** [`templates/client-report-template.md`](../templates/client-report-template.md).

## Principles

- **Lead with business outcomes** (leads, bookings, revenue, cost per lead), then platform metrics.
- **Use the client's numbers** where possible: CRM-qualified leads beat platform-reported conversions.
- **Compare like with like:** this month vs. last month and vs. the same month last year if seasonal.
- **Explain, don't list.** Every chart gets one sentence on what it means.
- **End with decisions:** what we'll do next month and what we need from the client.

## Process

1. **Day 1 to 3 of the month:** pull data for the previous full month. Wait for conversion lag on the last days to settle where it matters.
2. Fill sections 1 to 5 of the template.
3. Reconcile platform conversions with the client's CRM or bookings. Note any gap and its likely cause.
4. Write the summary last; it's three to five sentences.
5. Internal review: someone other than the account manager reads the summary and the next steps.
6. Send, and book a 20 to 30 minute review call for anything that needs a decision.

## Data sources

| Metric | Source |
|---|---|
| Spend, clicks, CPC, impressions | Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager |
| Leads / bookings | CRM or booking system first; platforms second |
| Revenue / ROAS | CRM or e-commerce platform |
| Site behaviour | GA4 |
| Organic | Search Console |

Looker Studio works well for the recurring data pages; keep the written summary and decisions in the document itself.
