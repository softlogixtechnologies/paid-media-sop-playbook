# SOP 03: Performance Max (lead gen and local)

**Goal:** add PMax reach without letting it cannibalise brand traffic or chase junk leads. **Prerequisite:** a working Search campaign with reliable, primary-only conversions.

## When to use it

- Use PMax once Search is converting steadily and you want reach across YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail and Maps.
- Hold off if conversion tracking includes soft events, if lead quality isn't fed back (offline conversions or CRM import), or if the budget can't support both Search and PMax.

PMax optimises toward whatever the conversion goal says is valuable. If the goal counts junk leads, PMax will find more junk leads.

## Setup

1. **Goal:** use only the lead or booking conversion actions. Prefer optimising to qualified leads imported from the CRM when available.
2. **Bidding:** Maximize Conversions to start; add a Target CPA once results stabilise.
3. **Brand protection:** apply brand exclusions so PMax doesn't take credit for brand searches your Brand Search campaign already captures.
4. **Negatives:** apply your account-level negative keyword list (it covers PMax) and check the campaign's search term insights after launch.
5. **Final URL expansion:** turn it off, or exclude URLs (careers, blog, legal pages) if left on, so PMax sends traffic only to pages built to convert.
6. **Locations:** same as Search, Presence only.

## Asset groups

One asset group per service theme. Each needs:

- Headlines, long headlines and descriptions written for that service (reuse the best-performing RSA messages).
- Real images: vehicles, team, locations. Avoid stock photos where possible.
- At least one video. Without one, Google may auto-generate a video from your images.
- Search themes that describe the service in the words customers use.
- An audience signal: customer list, website visitors, and custom segments built from your best search terms.

## Review cadence

- Weekly: conversions, CPA, and search term insights; add negatives at account level.
- Monthly: asset performance; replace low-rated assets with new ones rather than deleting everything at once.
- Compare Search + PMax combined CPA against Search alone before PMax launched. If total leads didn't rise, PMax is mostly re-labelling existing demand.
